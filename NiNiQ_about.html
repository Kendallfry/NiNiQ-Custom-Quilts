The Story Behind NiNi-Q

A lifelong love of design, expressed one quilt at a time.

Hello, I’m Jennifer—welcome to NiNi-Q Quilts. I’m so happy you’re here and excited to share my passion for quilting with you.

After graduating from Kent State University in 2015, I began my career designing everything from dancewear and intimate apparel to soft and hard accessories. Designing has always been at the heart of who I am, and every project has helped shape my creative journey.

While I love designing professionally, quilting is where my creativity truly comes alive. From selecting fabrics and cutting each piece to sewing every seam and arranging each block, every quilt is thoughtfully handcrafted with care and intention.

My hope is that each quilt becomes more than just a blanket. I want it to become a piece of art that brings warmth, comfort, and lasting memories into your home.

Whether you’re here to find your next quilt or simply looking for inspiration, thank you for visiting and supporting handmade craftsmanship.
