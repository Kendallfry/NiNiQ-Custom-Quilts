Privacy Policy

At NiNi Q Designs, your privacy is important. This policy explains what information may be collected through our website and how it is used.

Information We Collect

When you place an order, submit a contact or custom quilt request, or sign up for emails, we may collect information such as your name, email address, shipping address, order details, and any information you choose to provide.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only as needed to operate NiNi Q Designs, including to:

  • Process and fulfill orders
  • Respond to questions and custom quilt requests
  • Provide digital products
  • Send emails you have chosen to receive
  • Provide customer service
  • Improve and maintain the website

NiNi Q Designs does not sell your personal information.

Third-Party Services

NiNi Q Designs uses trusted third-party services to help operate the website, including services for forms, payments, shipping, email communications, and website functionality.

These providers may receive information necessary to perform their services and may have their own privacy policies.

Payments & Shipping

Payments are securely processed through the payment provider available at checkout. NiNi Q Designs does not directly store your complete credit or debit card information.

Information necessary to deliver your order may also be shared with USPS or another applicable shipping provider.

Email Communications

If you sign up to receive NiNi Q Designs emails, you may unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe option provided in the email or by contacting me.

Your Information

If you have questions about information you have provided, would like to correct it, or would like to request deletion where applicable, please contact NiNi Q Designs.

Certain information may need to be retained for legitimate transaction, tax, legal, or recordkeeping purposes.

Updates to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the NiNi Q Designs website and services evolve. Any updates will be posted on this page.
