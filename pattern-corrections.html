Still Have a Question?

If you don’t see your issue listed above or something in the pattern is still unclear, please reach out! I’m happy to help you get back on track.

When contacting me, please include:

  • The name of the pattern
  • The page or step you’re working on
  • A brief description of what’s unclear

Photos are always helpful if you’re having trouble explaining where you got stuck.
